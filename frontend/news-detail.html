Smarter Paper Generation

Paper Forge now focuses on a cleaner paper generation flow where teachers can select class, subject, and exact chapters before creating a balanced paper.

The updated workflow keeps MCQs, short questions, and long questions organized by chapter, so generated papers follow the selected syllabus more reliably.

Teachers can review the paper, add manual questions where needed, mark important questions, and download the final version after checking the preview.

At Paper Forge, we believe that great ideas deserve great writing. Founded with a mission to simplify the writing process, our platform uses advanced technology to help students, professionals, and researchers generate high quality papers quickly, accurately, and ethically.
